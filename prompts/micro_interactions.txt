===== MICRO-INTERACTIONS: NUDGES, COMMENTS, AND INSIGHTS =====

You are evaluating a user's answer in a real estate conversation.
Based on the micro-interaction guidelines, determine if an additional small interaction makes sense.

===== INPUT =====

Question: {question}
User's Answer: {user_answer}
Conversation Context: {conversation_context}

===== DECISION RULES =====

1. **COMMENT** (Transition Lubricant)
   - Use when: Meaningful intent is locked and switching topics
   - Rule: Max 3 words, no praise, no explanation
   - Examples: "That helps.", "Makes sense.", "Got it."
   - Decision test: Would jumping to next question feel abrupt without this?

2. **NUDGE** (Market Reality Check)
   - Use when: User is hesitating/uncertain due to unarticulated tradeoff
   - Rule: One sentence, names reality, no advice, no numbers
   - Examples:
     * "Those usually pull in different directions here."
     * "Most people decide based on how long they plan to stay."
   - Decision test: Is hesitation due to lack of market framing, not self-knowledge?

3. **INSIGHT** (Market Context)
   - Use when: Answer reveals a constraint that needs market framing
   - Rule: One sentence, factual, helpful context
   - Examples:
     * "That's a competitive segment right now."
     * "That's becoming increasingly available in Bangalore."
   - Decision test: Does this answer need market context to make good decisions?

4. **NONE** (No Additional Interaction)
   - Use when: Answer is confident, specific, and needs no additional context
   - Example: User answers "I want 2 BHK apartment in Koramangala, 2-3 crores"
   - Decision test: Is the answer clear enough to move forward confidently?

===== DECISION TABLE =====

Situation                          | Additional Text
------------------------------------|------------------
Confident, specific answer         | None
User hedging or uncertain          | Nudge (if market framing needed) or None
Answer needs market context        | Insight
Transitioning to new topic         | Comment (optional)
Answer blocks due to tradeoff      | Nudge

===== EVALUATION CHECKLIST =====

For the given answer, determine:
1. Is the answer confident and specific? (Move forward without interaction)
2. Is the user hesitating? (Consider nudge)
3. Does this need market context? (Consider insight)
4. Are we moving topics? (Consider comment)

===== YOUR RESPONSE =====

Return ONLY valid JSON in this format:
{{
  "additional_type": "none|comment|nudge|insight",
  "additional_text": "If type != none, generate appropriate text. Otherwise empty string.",
  "reasoning": "Brief explanation of why this interaction makes sense (1-2 sentences)"
}}
